# Option-specific acquisition: development experiment design

Hypothesis: the incumbent values a multi-option menu using an early deadline and a low revenue that can belong to different options. Restricting each candidate quote to one actual delivery option, with that option's revenue, deadline, dealer windows, route and fallback cost, may improve whole-week profit. It may instead lose valuable customer choice; that is a falsifying outcome, not a reason to tune prices after seeing results.

This is the first structural comparison in the bounded twelve-world tranche. It preserves the existing adaptive demand model, bundle-payment convention, fulfillment execution, solver, scenario assumptions and admission gates. It does not introduce the rejected fitted response models or claim that the existing fulfillment model is validated. It does not yet expand acquisition into private/trade-in work or plan two uncertain requests together. The broader architecture objective remains open.

Generate a separate proposal for every option already offered by the incumbent proposal. Keep alternative quote prices and existing-anchor route choices, but value each against its own option. The request remains one mutually exclusive resource: at most one quote can be selected. The real request, rather than a narrowed copy, stays in observed state. Dealer-site windows in synthetic awarded orders end at the chosen deadline; private slots are unchanged.

Enumerating options multiplies alternatives. The option arm allows at most360 alternatives rather than120, enough for the existing three requests, up to three options, existing anchor and payment choices. This is a declared ancillary change to avoid suppressing later requests simply because options are enumerated. The baseline remains unchanged; its previous8-week audit recorded no120-cap hits. Log omissions and solver failures; do not attribute a result solely to menu representation if truncation or solver behavior materially changes. Do not raise solver time limits in this study.

Before live execution, verify disabled-mode and single-option conformance, exact option identity/windows, option-dependent cost-cache separation, mutual exclusion, observed-state preservation and actual transmitted quote/replacement semantics. Preserve the historical frozen candidate. Do not modify or depend on concurrent untracked foundation files.

If preflight succeeds and official state/approval checks are available, freeze six familiar development seeds: the first six in level8-estimate-v1 manifest order. Run six new option-specific worlds against the six exact archived candidate outcomes. All attempts count; no replacements, no outcome-based stopping, no parameter sweep. At most8unfinished team worlds, shared lock and existing runner checks; unranked only. No experiment is launched by this design document.

Primary outcome: paired whole-week net profit. Companion outcomes: qualification, on-time share, orders, payments, refunds, undelivered work, failed visits, fines, action/runtime failures, candidate coverage and omissions. Report per-week and descriptive uncertainty. Development continuation requires at least€1,500/week mean gain, all6qualifying, minimum weekly on-time92%, and no undelivered orders, failed visits or fines. Passing does not establish superiority; it only supports a separately frozen next structural comparison and ultimately the untouched validation procedure. Failure retains the incumbent and records the mechanism's counterevidence.
