# Note-hours confirmation: not selected

The predeclared four-week development confirmation did not reproduce the screening gain. Mean margin fell **€274.73/week**, with two positive and two negative pairs. All four treatments qualified, but the feature fails the protocol's profitability and consistency criteria and remains disabled.

| Old development seed | Bundle-only margin | Hours margin | Gain |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 09 | €8,145.27 | €8,507.71 | €362.44 |
| 10 | €13,173.80 | €12,312.79 | −€861.01 |
| 12 | €11,773.52 | €10,952.93 | −€820.59 |
| 16 | €9,547.79 | €9,768.05 | €220.26 |

Among 86 noncancelled parsed-hours orders, three were late, none had a failed visit, and none remained undelivered. One reprice was rejected because its gig was no longer open; the complete action and world outcome are retained. Minimum whole-week on-time share was 94.71%.

The comparison uses the explicitly archived bundle-only controls. The hours source adds opt-in parsing and awaited expiry handling; disabled-hours controls on seeds 14 and 15 reproduced the archived results to the cent. This source difference is declared in the [protocol](../experiments/note-hours-confirmation-protocol.json), rather than presented as a byte-identical code contrast.

The [machine-readable report](note-hours-confirmation.json) contains every planned outcome, exact source/settings, ledger reconciliation, delivery counts, provenance hashes and the hours-order audit. All four worlds finished. No fresh frontier evaluation seed was used here, and no ranking submission was made.

Accurate parsing expands the feasible request set, but does not establish that accepting the extra work raises whole-week profit. The bundle-only source remains the best independently validated candidate.
