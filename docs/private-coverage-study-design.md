# Private appointment coverage study

The incumbent excludes private-address work. An arrival-time graph screen on eight familiar weeks found786plain-note, single-leg private requests with a feasible option and180minutes of pickup slack after response. This establishes exposure only. Public-job fulfillment estimates and an absence of historical private failures do not establish private-job profitability or reliability.

Hypothesis: a bounded subset can win at a conservative menu price and complete inside its private appointment, with measurable contribution. Randomize eligible arrivals with probability1/2 each to neutral decline or a menu quote. Preserve all graph-feasible options with180minutes slack; never extend a private window. Quote every option at a common ratio equal to max(1.4,1.35baseFee/minimum offered list price), rounded up in cents. No private quote outcome updates the public demand model: pricing is fixed, and these observations are reserved for their own support analysis. Dealer trust and capacity state still respond to real outcomes.

Eligibility: level8, one leg, at least one private stop, a plain fully supported note, graph feasibility and booking slack. Unknown notes, timed-note private appointments and trade-ins remain excluded. Maximum24assignments/week and4/day, counting declines; no assignment while two exploratory quotes/orders are pending or unbooked. A booked driver dropping later can increase that exposure without new admission. After any visit failure on exploratory work, cease further exploratory assignments for that week while fulfilling existing obligations. This stopping rule is part of the frozen policy, not a reason to erase its outcome. Inherited posting, booking, rescue and equipment rules remain; private orders are not eligible for the existing bundle helper.

Freeze eight fresh development seeds and both arms before play: sixteen new unranked worlds, one byte-preserved incumbent and one coverage world per seed, alternating launch order. At most four own runs and eight unfinished team worlds under the shared creation lock.600second world runtime. Stop new creation on runtime failure, preserve active outcomes and all partial data; no retries, replacements, deletion, ranking or finals. Seeds are retired from untouched validation before execution. Complete the fixed panel before economic interpretation.

For every private arrival log eligibility, budget/exposure exclusions and decision-time request features. For every assignment retain probability, arm, all offered options and prices, source windows, slack, base fee, exposure and successful/failed quote acknowledgment. Retain wins, losses, cancellations, appointment outcomes and all ledger lines. Lost quotes and control assignments have observed zero direct contribution; unknown actions/outcomes remain unknown. Do not assign shared driver fees arbitrarily if later changes form a bundle: expose unallocated amounts and affected observations.

Primary local estimand: assignment effect on realized request contribution under the exploratory policy, conditional on its sequential eligibility history. Report inverse-probability weighted totals and per-week arm means, with eight-week descriptive uncertainty. Assignments interact through capacity, learning and trust; per-order differences do not identify independent marginal capacity values. Compare the full package with its matched incumbent on whole-week profit, qualification, throughput, fees, refunds, undelivered work, failed visits, fines and action/runtime failures. No order-level confidence claim may replace week-level evidence.

Minimum support for subsequent model checks: at least12assignments per arm over at least3weeks and at least10observed wins. Meeting those counts permits development analysis only. First compare a constant conditional fee baseline, one fixed small model using route distance, appointment slack, base fee and eligible supply, and the reconstructible incumbent heuristic using whole-seed holdouts; no hyperparameter search. Apply the existing cost gate of10%held-out error improvement and6of8week wins over both baselines. This panel does not identify a price elasticity or customer menu-choice model because it uses one quote rule. If support fails, retain the evidence and do not fit or scale acquisition.

This is collection, not a replacement or promotion experiment. No outcome of these eight reused-after-play development weeks can establish leaderboard superiority. Any structural policy derived from the data needs its own frozen whole-policy comparison, followed by untouched validation under existing multiplicity,24-pair minimum and reliability requirements. The screen's apparent coverage must not be treated as predicted profit.
