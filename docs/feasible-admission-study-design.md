# Feasible candidate admission experiment

Hypothesis: the inherited pre-optimization admission gate excludes profitable, physically feasible public single-leg requests. Replacing that gate for a bounded subset with explicit feasible menu candidates lets the existing portfolio choose acquisition jointly with fulfillment and capacity. Preserve every incumbent proposal exactly. This is a candidate-coverage intervention, not a fitted cost-model replacement.

For incumbent-rejected requests only, require a plain supported note, one public leg, no private stops, and at least180minutes of feasible pickup slack after the response deadline. Use the tested graph for physical feasibility. Retain all eligible delivery options in one menu; the conservative planning deadline and revenue are the earliest deadline and smallest quoted price. Preserve adaptive demand, fee convention, fulfillment scenarios, capacity and risk constraints, rescue policy and120alternative cap. At most two expansion orders may be pending or unbooked simultaneously. Expansion awards count against ordinary capacity too. Do not change the global cost scale following the selected accepted-work diagnostic.

The expansion removes legacy home-supply, distance and admission-buffer vetoes only where graph feasibility and the new bound permit a candidate. It does not support unknown notes, private work, trade-ins, or uncertain two-new-request routes. Home-supply capacity remains a model limitation; this experiment cannot validate it. Existing coverage included30 exploratory awards, but those local interventions did not identify displacement effects or justify broad expansion.

Freeze six new unranked treatment worlds on the first six level8-estimate-v1 seeds, compared with their exact archived portfolio-payments outcomes. These familiar seeds are development only. This completes the remaining six-world structural tranche; no outcome-dependent repeats, replacement, tuning or promotion. Use at most four own workers, at most eight unfinished team worlds and the shared creation lock. Preserve600second world budgets; runtime failure stops new creation while active cases retain outcomes. No ranked or final actions.

Primary outcome: paired whole-week net profit. Report all qualification, on-time, throughput, fee/refund, undelivered, failed-visit, fine, action-error and runtime outcomes with descriptive paired-week uncertainty. Report expansion candidates, quotes, awards, exclusions and binding exposure limits; successful runtime without actual expansion does not exercise the hypothesis. Development gate: mean paired gain at least€1,500/week, all six qualifying, minimum weekly on-time92%, no undelivered orders, failed visits, fines or runtime failures. Failure retains the incumbent. Passing only supports a separately frozen ablation and untouched validation under the existing24-pair floor and multiplicity/reliability rules. Never interpret unmatched leaderboard scores as a paired superiority test.
