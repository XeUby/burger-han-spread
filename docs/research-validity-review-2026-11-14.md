# Research validity review and response

The researcher’s assessment is accepted: this work is structured exploratory research with stronger reproducibility than scientific validation. Frozen manifests, seed matching, failure preservation and solver tests do not establish that the economic objective is correct or that the dispatcher is competitive.

The unresolved requirements are decision-valid booking/cost/capacity/option-choice models, supported structural hypotheses, strong simple baselines, and one frozen finalist tested on a sufficiently large untouched weekly panel. Solver optimality remains a statement about the supplied finite model. The payment ablation corrected an attribution error; it did not validate the architecture.

At the time of this review, `coverage-development-v1` had been source-frozen but had not launched. Before execution, `coverage-development-v2` added the hashed identification plan in `docs/coverage-identification-plan.md`. The policy, eight familiar seeds, randomly assigned weekly regimes and per-week randomization seeds were unchanged. v1 remains an unlaunched record. The v2 protocol adds hypotheses, estimands, attribution/censoring rules, minimum-support conditions, simple predictive baselines, held-out development screens and a separate structural/final-validation sequence.

The launched study is data collection. It cannot establish profitable private/trade-in acquisition, optimal offer targeting, capacity opportunity cost, customer menu choice, economic-model validity or competitive superiority. It may identify adequate support for narrower model checks—or show that more targeted interventions are required. Failed actions, sparse acceptance events, collapsed realized price arms and incomplete cost attribution can invalidate an intended analysis even when every world completes.

No policy promotion, ranking authorization or validation exception follows from the study. The full competitive objective remains open.

## Executed evidence and deviation

All eight coverage worlds completed and qualified, but the package lost €482.66/week relative to the archived candidate. It produced192 acquisition assignments and320 randomized direct-post openings. A post-execution audit found that batched actions bypassed the dispatcher proxy:2,097 offers were sent with no randomized offer assignments. Thus H3 was not executed and H2 covers a restricted direct-action subset. The report explicitly flags protocol nonconformance. The source correction and a practice-batch regression test are retained separately from the immutable executed bundle. No failed hypothesis is relabeled as successful data collection.

This is concrete evidence for the reviewer’s distinction: successful tests and completed worlds did not prove that the intended intervention reached the live execution path. Further checks must establish both intervention delivery and economic validity. Current evidence supports restricted acquisition/booking model diagnostics; it does not support offer-price inference or deployment of the exploratory package.
